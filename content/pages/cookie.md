**Last updated: 23 September 2026**

This page lists what MidiDraft stores in your browser and why.

## Strictly necessary

These are needed for the site to work and cannot be turned off without breaking
it.

| Purpose | What it stores | Lifetime |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sign-in session | A session token, if you have an account and are signed in | Until you sign out, or the session expires |
| Security | A token used to protect form submissions | Session |
| Theme | Whether you chose light or dark mode | Persistent, on your device only |

## Analytics

We use Google Analytics to see which pages are visited and whether conversions
start, finish or fail. It is configured with Google signals and ad
personalisation turned off, so it does not track you across other websites and
does not build an advertising profile.

| Cookie | Set by | Purpose | Lifetime |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `_ga` | Google Analytics | Tells one visitor's repeat visits apart | 2 years |
| `_ga_<id>` | Google Analytics | Keeps the current session's state | 2 years |

It never records the audio you convert, the file name, or the MIDI produced.
Those never leave your device, so they are not available to any analytics
system.

## What is not here

- No advertising or retargeting cookies.
- No social media tracking pixels.
- No cross-site profiling.
- No storage of your audio or MIDI data, in cookies or anywhere else.

## Local storage used by the converter

The converter caches the transcription model — about 1 MB — in your browser so
the second conversion starts faster than the first. This is a model file, not
data about you, and clearing your browser cache removes it.

## Controlling cookies

You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings. Blocking the necessary
ones will sign you out and may stop parts of the site working. The converter
itself does not require cookies.
