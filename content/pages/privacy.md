**Last updated: 23 September 2026**

This policy describes how MidiDraft ("we", "the site") handles information. It
is written to be checkable against what the site actually does, not to cover
every theoretical case.

## Who is responsible

MidiDraft is built and run by an independent developer, who is responsible
for the data the site collects. Privacy questions and requests go to
[mididraft@outlook.com](mailto:mididraft@outlook.com).

Purchases are handled differently: **Waffo Pancake** is the merchant of record
for every order and processes your payment and billing data as an independent
controller. See [Payments](#payments) below.

## The short version

- Your audio is converted in your browser. It is never uploaded to us, never
  stored by us, and never seen by us.
- We collect anonymous usage data about how the converter performs.
- If you create an account, we store your name, email and sign-in records.
- If you buy a plan, Waffo Pancake takes the payment. We never see your card
  details.
- We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for
  advertising.

## Your audio and MIDI files

When you convert a file:

- The file is read directly from your device by your browser.
- The transcription model runs on your machine, inside the page.
- The resulting MIDI file is generated on your device and saved by your browser
  when you click download.

No part of your audio, and no part of the resulting MIDI, is transmitted to our
servers or to any third party. We never see the file name, the file contents,
or the notes that come out. Because we never receive a copy, there is nothing
for you to ask us to delete — closing the tab ends it.

This applies to the converter as it exists today. If a future feature requires
server-side processing, it will be clearly labelled as such before you use it,
it will be opt-in, and this policy will be updated first.

## Usage data

We use **Google Analytics** to measure whether the tool works for people. It
records:

- Which pages were visited, and the referring site.
- Whether a conversion was started, completed, cancelled or failed, and the
  category of any error (for example "decode failed" or "out of memory").
- Coarse properties of a conversion: the file format (for example "wav"), a
  length range (for example "15–30s"), how long processing took, and which
  settings groups were touched.
- Device type, browser, and approximate country, which Google derives from your
  IP address.

It does **not** receive file names, audio, note data, or anything that
identifies the material you converted. We have turned off Google signals and
ad personalisation, so this data is not linked to your Google account or used
for advertising. Analytics data is kept for 14 months. Google's handling is
described in its [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Account data

You can use the converter without an account. If you create one, we store:

- The name and email address you give us, or that Google shares with us if you
  sign in with Google (name, email and profile picture).
- Your password, stored only as a one-way hash.
- Sign-in sessions, including the IP address and browser they were created
  from, so we can keep you signed in and spot misuse of your account.

We use your email to run your account: email verification and password
resets. We only send product news if you subscribe to it from your account
settings, and every such email has an unsubscribe link.

## Payments

When you buy a Project Pass or a Pro subscription, the checkout is hosted by
**Waffo Pancake** (waffo.ai), our merchant of record. Waffo sells the plan to
you on our behalf: it collects the payment, calculates and remits sales tax or
VAT, issues your invoice or receipt, and handles refunds and chargebacks. To do
that it processes your payment method, billing name, billing address and email
under its own privacy policy. Your card details go to Waffo and its payment
partners, never to us.

From Waffo we receive a notice of each order. We keep what we need to give you
access: your email, the plan you bought, the order and payment identifiers, the
status (paid, renewed, cancelled, refunded) and the access or subscription
period. We keep that with your account for as long as the account exists.
Waffo keeps its own transaction records for as long as tax law requires.
Subscription management, invoices and billing details are available in Waffo's
customer portal.

## Cookies

The site sets cookies that it needs to work — keeping you signed in and
remembering your light or dark theme — and, where analytics is enabled, Google
Analytics cookies that tell repeat visits apart. See the
[Cookie Policy](/cookie) for the full list.

## Who else processes data

| Provider | What for | Data involved |
|-|-|-|
| Cloudflare | Hosting, database, email delivery | Everything the site stores; request logs |
| Google | Analytics; "Sign in with Google" if you use it | Usage data as above; your Google profile basics |
| Waffo Pancake | Checkout, billing, tax, invoices (merchant of record) | Payment and billing details |
| Resend | Product news, only if you subscribe | Your email address |
| Discord | Internal alert to us when a purchase is made | Your name and email, the order and amount |

None of them receive your audio, because it never reaches us. These providers
may process data outside your country, including in the United States; where
the law requires it, transfers rely on the providers' standard contractual
safeguards.

We disclose personal data beyond this list only when the law requires it.

## How long we keep it

| Data | Kept for |
|-|-|
| Account, sign-in records and purchase records | Until you delete your account |
| Analytics data | 14 months |
| Server request logs | A few days, by our host |
| Emails you send us | As long as needed to answer, and at most 2 years |

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export
or delete the personal data we hold about you, to object to or restrict certain
processing, and to withdraw consent. Email
[mididraft@outlook.com](mailto:mididraft@outlook.com) and we will respond within 30
days. You can also delete your account yourself from your account settings,
which removes your profile, sign-in records and our copy of your purchase
records. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

For payment and invoice data, Waffo is the controller; you can reach it through
its customer portal, and we will help if you ask us.

## Security

The site is served over HTTPS only, passwords are hashed, and access to the
database is limited to the operator. If a breach affects your personal data, we
will notify you and the relevant authority as the law requires, within 72 hours
of discovering it where that applies.

## Children

The site is not directed at children under 13, or under the minimum age for
online services in your country if that is higher. We do not knowingly collect
data from them; if you believe a child has given us data, email us and we will
delete it.

## Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects how your data is handled, the date
at the top will change and material changes will be noted on the site.
