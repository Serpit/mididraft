**Last updated: 22 September 2026**

This policy describes how MidiDraft ("we", "the site") handles information. It
is written to be checkable against what the site actually does, not to cover
every theoretical case.

## The short version

Your audio files are converted in your browser. They are never uploaded to us,
never stored by us, and never seen by us. We collect a small amount of
anonymous usage data about how the converter performs, and account data only if
you choose to create an account.

## Your audio and MIDI files

When you convert a file:

- The file is read directly from your device by your browser.
- The transcription model runs on your machine, inside the page.
- The resulting MIDI file is generated on your device and saved by your browser
  when you click download.

No part of your audio, and no part of the resulting MIDI, is transmitted to our
servers or to any third party. We never see the file name, the file contents,
or the notes that come out. Because we never receive a copy, there is nothing
for you to ask us to delete — closing the tab ends it.

This applies to the free converter as it exists today. If a future feature
requires server-side processing, it will be clearly labelled as such before you
use it, it will be opt-in, and this policy will be updated first.

## Usage data

We collect anonymous, aggregated data about how the site is used, so we can
tell whether the tool actually works for people. This can include:

- Which pages were visited, and the referring site.
- Whether a conversion was started, completed, cancelled, or failed.
- The category of an error (for example "decode failed" or "out of memory").
- Approximate duration of a conversion, and whether it ran on desktop or
  mobile.
- Approximate country, derived from your IP address and not stored alongside it.

We do **not** record file names, audio content, note data, or anything that
identifies the material you converted.

## Account data

You can use the converter without an account. If you create one, we store the
email address and name you provide, and authentication records needed to keep
you signed in. If you make a purchase, our payment provider processes your
payment details — we receive a record that a payment occurred and its status,
never your card number.

You can delete your account from your account settings. Deleting it removes
your profile and authentication records. Records we are required to keep for
tax or accounting purposes are retained for as long as the law requires.

## Cookies

The site uses cookies that are necessary for it to work — keeping you signed in
and remembering your light or dark theme preference. Analytics, where used, is
configured not to build cross-site profiles. See the
[Cookie Policy](/cookie) for detail.

## Third parties

We use service providers for hosting, email, analytics and payments. They
process data on our behalf under their own terms. None of them receive your
audio, because your audio never reaches us in the first place.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export
or delete the personal data we hold about you, and to object to certain
processing. Email us and we will action it. For audio files there is nothing to
action, because we hold none.

## Children

The site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
data from them.

## Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects how your data is handled, the date
at the top will change and material changes will be noted on the site.
