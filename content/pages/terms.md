## What the service is

MidiDraft converts audio you supply into a MIDI file, in your browser. The
output is a **draft**: an approximation of the notes in your recording,
intended as a starting point you edit in your own software.

We do not claim, and you should not assume, that the output is an accurate
transcription. Accuracy varies enormously with the material. Clear recordings
of a single instrument convert reasonably well; dense mixes, heavy effects and
fast passages do not. Examples of both are published on the
[examples page](/examples) so you can judge before relying on it.

This is a statement of what the tool does, not a disclaimer used in place of
quality. If it does not produce something useful for your material, do not pay
for it.
